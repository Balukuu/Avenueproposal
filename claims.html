Claims Services

Our Philosophy

Professional Claims Management

Claims processing forms an integral part of our broking services and we appreciate the fact that the best way to demonstrate professional broking services is by ensuring prompt and adequate compensation should a loss occur.

This philosophy has enabled us retain most if not all our corporate and individual clients over the years. We understand that insurance represents a promise to pay, and we are here to ensure that promise is fulfilled.

The Process

How we service claims

Below is a detailed analysis of how we normally service claims for our esteemed clients.

Step 1: Reporting

Immediate advice on what action to take when a loss occurs. We guide you through the initial notification to insurers.

Step 2: Analysis

Our claims department personnel conduct a detailed analysis of the loss and policy coverage.

Step 3: Documentation

We assist in preparing and collating all required documentation to support your claim effectively.

Step 4: Settlement

Prompt follow-up with underwriters to ensure smooth and adequate compensation for the loss.
